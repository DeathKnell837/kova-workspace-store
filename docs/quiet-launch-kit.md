## Draft site copy for an affiliate pivot

**Disclosure:** “KOVA may earn a commission when you shop through some links. The price you pay does not change. We only link to products available from the retailer; check the retailer’s current price, specifications, delivery estimate, and return terms before buying.”

**Collection intro:** “A practical edit of desk organization finds, chosen to help make small workspaces feel easier to use. Product availability and delivery are handled by the retailer.”

Use these only after affiliate approval and after adding valid tracked links. Clearly label retailer links. Remove the store’s direct-payment, bag, and order-tracking flow for affiliate items, because the retailer—not KOVA—takes payment and handles fulfillment.

## Product review checklist before publishing a recommendation

- Confirm the retailer listing is active and the exact variant, material, measurements, and price are visible.
- Use permitted product photos and tracked links from the approved affiliate dashboard.
- Say when a recommendation contains an affiliate link; never imply KOVA stocks or ships affiliate products.
- Avoid unverified health, ergonomic, durability, savings, or performance promises.
- Recheck the link and listing before posting; retailers can change price, availability, and terms.
