What to check in a laptop stand

Listings can look alike while fitting different devices. Check the details that determine whether a stand will work on your desk before comparing style or price.

A practical DeskLaya guide · Updated October 2026

Fit and dimensions

  • Compare the stated supported laptop size or weight with your device's specifications.
  • Check the platform width and depth, including any lips or raised edges.
  • Measure desk space and the stand's full footprint, including the base.
  • Confirm the height and angle adjustment range if the stand is adjustable.

Stability and construction

Look for the material, base design, hinge or locking details, and care instructions. Read recent buyer reviews for reports about wobbling or fit, but treat individual reviews as experiences rather than guarantees. A listing photo alone cannot establish stability.

Ventilation and access

Check whether the stand blocks vents, ports, or the charging connection on your specific laptop. Avoid covering ventilation openings. If the stand includes a fan or electronics, review power requirements, compatibility, warranty, and local support; a simple non-powered model is easier to compare.

Price and delivery details

Compare the current total at checkout, delivery estimate to your location, seller information, return window, and warranty terms. These can change and may differ by seller or variant. Do not rely on a price in an old article or search result.

Questions to answer before adding one

  1. What problem should the stand solve in my current setup?
  2. Does the listing show the exact measurements I need?
  3. Will I still have room for a keyboard, mouse, or notebook?
  4. Can I return it if the fit is wrong?
