How KOVA works

KOVA is preparing its first retailer recommendations. The site currently does not take product orders or payments. Do not send KOVA payment or delivery details.

Affiliate links

If KOVA is approved for an affiliate program and publishes tracked links, those links will be clearly labeled. KOVA may earn a commission if a reader shops through one. The retailer sets the price and handles checkout, payment, fulfillment, delivery, cancellations, returns, and refunds. KOVA does not claim to stock or ship retailer products.

Prices and product information

Any future product notes will be based on the retailer listing and checked at the time of publication. Retailer prices, stock, seller details, specifications, shipping coverage, and return terms can change. Confirm the latest details on the retailer's page before buying. KOVA will distinguish listing research from hands-on product testing.

Privacy

This informational site does not currently offer an account, newsletter, or KOVA checkout form. If you follow a retailer link, that retailer may collect information under its own privacy terms. Do not send personal or payment information to KOVA through this site.

Contact and consumer information

KOVA has not published a customer-support contact because it is not currently taking orders. For a purchase from a retailer, use the contact and complaint channels shown by that retailer. For general Philippine e-commerce consumer guidance, see the Department of Trade and Industry eCommerce FAQ.
